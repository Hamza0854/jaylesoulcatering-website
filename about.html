ABOUT
CHEF JAY LeSOUL

Cooking with soul for 35+ years

Born in Boston, Massachusetts, and rooted in Cleveland, Ohio — serving great food and giving back to the community.

Professionally known as Chef Jay LeSoul, James Thomas Garrett III was born in Boston and has called Cleveland home since his youth. For more than 35 years, he has brought people together through food. As a Cleveland-based chef and community advocate, he combines soulful cooking, bold flavors, fresh ingredients, and a genuine passion for service.

Through Chef Jay LeSoul Catering, he has served more than 15,000 meals and created memorable dining experiences for private clients, community gatherings, special events, and distinguished guests. His culinary experience includes preparing meals for Senator Portman and serving members of the legendary R&B group The Whispers.

MORE THAN A CHEF — A COMMITMENT TO COMMUNITY

Chef Jay believes food is more than a meal. It is an opportunity to nourish, educate, encourage, and bring people together.

Serving the Community: Volunteering at local food banks to help address hunger and support neighbors in need.

Empowering Seniors: Sharing practical cooking techniques with seniors to build confidence and enjoyment in the kitchen.

Inspiring Youth: Participating in youth literacy initiatives that help young people strengthen reading skills through recipes, cooking instructions, and menus.

Cooking in the Hood: Championing the belief that great food, culinary knowledge, and opportunity belong in every neighborhood.

THE CHEF BEHIND THE SOUL

With a dedicated team of 10, Chef Jay brings experience, creativity, and personal attention to every event. Whether preparing an intimate dinner, corporate function, or community celebration, his mission remains the same: deliver exceptional food with heart.

His work reflects a simple philosophy: great cooking feeds the body, but giving back feeds the soul.

HIS MISSION

To create unforgettable culinary experiences, share knowledge across generations, serve the community, and inspire the next generation through food, education, and opportunity.

The Story Behind Black Crack Seasoning

Created by Chef Jay LeSoul

Every great flavor has a story, and Black Crack Seasoning was born in a kitchen filled with good food, great company, and unforgettable flavor!

It all started when Chef Jay LeSoul prepared a fish fry for a gathering of guests. His signature blend of herbs and spices created a bold, mouthwatering flavor that had everyone talking. The guests loved it so much that Chef Jay knew he had something special on his hands.

Inspired by their reaction, he turned his signature recipe into a bottled seasoning. And just like that, Black Crack Seasoning was born!

Black Crack brings bold, savory flavor to every bite, transforming ordinary meals into something extraordinary. Sprinkle it on crispy fried fish, juicy chicken, tender beef, fresh vegetables, or golden French fries for a flavorful touch that keeps you coming back for more.

What began as a fish fry favorite is now a seasoning made to bring excitement, flavor, and soul to kitchens everywhere.
